Privacy

Privacy

Last updated September 20, 2026

Draft. This page is a working draft written for the store. Have it reviewed against your own practices and local law before you take payment, then set legalDraft to false to remove this note.

What we collect

  • When you order: your email, name, shipping address and phone number (collected by Stripe at checkout and passed to us), and the name, start date and time zone you chose for each book.
  • When you write to us: your name, email and message.
  • When you subscribe: your email address.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. This site does not use advertising trackers.

Who handles it

  • Stripe processes payment and collects your shipping details. Your card number goes to Stripe and never to us.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores our order queue, messages and email list.
  • Our print partner receives your name, shipping address, phone number and email so it can print and deliver your book.

On your device

Your cart is kept in your browser’s local storage so it is still there if you come back. It is not sent to us until you check out.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as we need them for support, tax and accounting. Messages are kept until we have answered them. Your email stays on the list until you ask us to remove it.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by writing through the contact page. Ask to leave the email list the same way.

Children

This store is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we change this page we will update the date above.

Peace be with you.

A short note when a new volume or edition ships. Nothing else, and you can leave at any time.
